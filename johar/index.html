    Frequently Asked Questions

    Q. Kya consultation sirf women ke liye hai? +
    This landing page is specifically designed for women seeking consultation for piles, fissure, fistula and related digestive concerns in a comfortable, female-led environment.
    Q. Mujhe apni problem explain karne mein embarrassment hoti hai. +
    Bilkul understandable hai. Aapko apni symptoms ko openly discuss karne ka comfortable environment milna chahiye. Consultation ka purpose hi aapki concern ko respectfully samajhna aur appropriate next step discuss karna hai.
    Q. Kya har piles/fissure patient ko same treatment diya jata hai? +
    Nahi. Har patient ke symptoms, severity aur lifestyle circumstances different ho sakte hain. Isliye personalized consultation aur tailored guidance zaroori hoti hai.
    Q. Kya Unani/Ayurvedic care mere liye suitable hogi? +
    Ye individual condition par depend karta hai. Consultation ke dauran aapki symptoms aur health history discuss karke appropriate wellness approach determine ki ja sakti hai.
    Q. Kya main pehle sirf consultation le sakti hoon? +
    Yes, absolutely. Aap pehle private consultation lekar apni problem discuss kar sakti hain aur recommended next steps samajh sakti hain.
    Q. Agar mujhe bleeding ho rahi ho toh? +
    Bleeding ko ignore nahi karna chahiye. Rectal bleeding ke multiple causes ho sakte hain, isliye proper evaluation important hai.
    ⚠️ Emergency Note: Heavy bleeding, severe acute pain, high fever, dizziness ya fainting jaise rapidly worsening symptoms mein turant nearest hospital / urgent emergency care seek karein.
